# Document productivity synthesis matrix

Baseline: 11 ranked hashes, 7 distinct names.

Evidence labels: `pdf`, `pptx`, `xlsx`, `docx`, `doc-coauthoring`, `internal-comms`, `obsidian-markdown`.

| Decision area | Retained synthesis | Conflict resolution or added safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native structure | Use real headings, formulas, charts, fields, links, and notes | Visual imitation does not count as editable structure |
| PDF/document work | Preserve layout, semantics, comments, and safe redaction | The visual page and package structure both require validation |
| Spreadsheets | Separate inputs, calculations, and outputs; recalculate and reconcile | Displayed values alone do not prove formula integrity |
| Presentations | Build an audience decision and one claim per slide | Slide density is governed by projected readability, not document-page norms |
| Workplace writing | Lead with outcome, evidence, risk, owner, and action | Polishing cannot invent consensus, dates, owners, or facts |
| Knowledge notes | Follow vault conventions and create durable contextual links | Specialized syntax is used only when it adds semantic or operational value |
| Omission repair | Added rendering, portability, accessibility, and conversion disclosures | Source-package inspection alone cannot prove the final artifact |

Resulting modes: documents/PDF, spreadsheets, presentations, workplace writing, and knowledge Markdown.
