# Reasoning modes synthesis matrix

Baseline: 6 ranked hashes, 4 distinct names.

Evidence labels: `grill-me`, `brainstorming`, `zoom-out`, `caveman`.

| Decision area | Retained synthesis | Conflict resolution or added safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Activation | Require a clear user signal for the chosen mode | Potential usefulness does not authorize confrontational or compressed interaction |
| Brainstorming | Inspect known context, refine interactively, diverge across mechanisms, then compare and converge | Design gates are used when requested, not imposed on every creative task |
| Adversarial review | Probe consequential assumptions, follow weak answers, and traverse dependent decisions | Pressure targets claims, never the person; inspect facts rather than quizzing the user |
| Zooming out | Change abstraction level, map relevant callers/owners, and return the insight to action | Reframing cannot become avoidance |
| Compression | Preserve outcome, reason, action, and material warning | Brevity cannot erase risk or fabricate certainty |
| Authority | Preserve domain workflows, permissions, and evidence | A mode changes conversation behavior only |
| Omission repair | Added exit conditions, consolidation, and mode-composition rules | Personas do not persist after the user changes the interaction |

Resulting modes: brainstorming, adversarial review, zoom-out, and compressed communication.
