# Evidence pack: RelayDesk

RelayDesk helps small operations teams coordinate recurring handoffs.

Known facts:

- 42 active trial accounts: 24 logistics consultancies, 11 local service companies, and 7 other small businesses.
- Logistics consultancies complete setup at 58%; local service companies complete setup at 27%.
- Eight logistics trials connected three or more client workspaces; no other segment did.
- Five unsolicited emails ask for client-level permissions and consolidated reporting.
- Current plan is one flat monthly fee. Gross margin and support cost by segment have not been calculated.
- Two prospects said a competitor was “too enterprise,” but stated willingness to pay was not measured and no purchase followed.
- The product has no annual contract, migration tooling, or published service-level commitment.

Unknown:

- Addressable market size
- Segment retention beyond eight weeks
- Willingness to pay
- Support cost and gross margin by account shape
- Conversion effect of multiple packages
