# Expected decisions

- Prioritize a segment from observed urgency and product fit rather than generic persona language.
- Express positioning against the customer's current alternative.
- Treat packaging and value metric as hypotheses when economics and willingness-to-pay evidence are missing.
- Avoid numeric price recommendations from competitor anecdotes alone.
- Specify a conversion event and trust, support, and retention guardrails.
- Define disconfirming evidence and the downstream handoff to interface and application engineering.
