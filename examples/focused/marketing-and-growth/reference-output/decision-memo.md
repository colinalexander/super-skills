# Reference commercial decision memo

## Decision

Prioritize small logistics consultancies managing several client workspaces. The supplied evidence shows stronger setup completion, multi-workspace use, and unsolicited demand for permissions and reporting. This is a product-fit signal, not proof of market size or willingness to pay.

## Positioning hypothesis

For logistics consultancies coordinating recurring client handoffs, RelayDesk provides a simpler multi-client operating view than enterprise workflow suites or spreadsheet-and-email coordination.

## Package hypothesis

Test a single-workspace package against a multi-client package differentiated by workspace count, client permissions, and consolidated reporting. Do not set numeric prices until gross margin, support cost, current commitments, and willingness-to-pay evidence are available.

## Smallest credible test

Interview active and lost logistics trials, calculate account economics, and show a non-binding package concept to qualified prospects. Primary evidence: qualified package preference followed by a concrete next step. Guardrails: setup completion, support burden, trust, and early retention.

Reject or revise the segment focus if multi-workspace behavior does not persist, permission/reporting demand does not affect purchase behavior, or segment economics are unattractive.
