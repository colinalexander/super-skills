# Marketing and growth: positioning and pricing test

This example develops a commercial decision from a deliberately incomplete evidence pack without inventing customers, market size, or numeric prices.

- **Trigger:** segment, positioning, packaging, pricing evidence, and measurable conversion behavior are central.
- **Non-trigger:** visual design and implementation of a pricing page belong to other skills.
- **Fixture:** [`fixture/evidence.md`](fixture/evidence.md).
- **Reference artifact:** [`reference-output/decision-memo.md`](reference-output/decision-memo.md).
- **Verification:** trace every claim to supplied evidence, label assumptions, and define outcomes that could reject the recommendation.
