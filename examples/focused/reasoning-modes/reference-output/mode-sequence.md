# Reference mode sequence

## Explore

Distinct mechanisms include programming for remote workers, partnerships with nearby schools or services, improved free work amenities, appointment-based specialist help, and paid space rental. Compare them by mission fit, staffing load, reversibility, evidence, and weekday demand.

## Adversarial review

First question: What evidence shows that the six Wi-Fi requests represent sustained demand for paid space rather than demand for a better free library service?

## Zoom out

The objective may not be “increase door count.” It may be “create more weekday public value within the library's mission and staffing constraint.” That reframing keeps free access and service quality in the decision.

## Compress

Do not build paid coworking yet. Test free work improvements and demand first. Protect quiet space and public access.

## Current decision

Run a reversible four-week free-work pilot and survey users before considering paid conversion. The evidence is currently too weak to justify permanent space or pricing decisions.
