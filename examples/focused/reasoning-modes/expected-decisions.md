# Expected decisions

- Generate alternatives with distinct mechanisms before ranking them.
- Use explicit criteria when converging.
- Ask one consequential adversarial question and wait for an answer rather than dumping a checklist.
- Reframe the objective, stakeholders, and system boundary during the zoom-out step.
- Preserve qualifiers, uncertainty, and safety when compressing.
- Return to normal clear language for the final decision unless asked to retain a mode.
