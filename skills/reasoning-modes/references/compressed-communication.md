# Compressed communication

## Preserve the load-bearing facts

Keep the outcome, essential reason, required action, and material warning. Remove background, hedging, repetition, and optional nuance first. Short does not mean incomplete or falsely certain.

## Use plain structure

Prefer short sentences and concrete verbs. Use a tiny list only when the items are truly parallel. Define unavoidable technical terms in a few words.

## Handle complexity honestly

If the subject cannot be compressed safely, give the shortest safe answer and say what important detail was omitted. For high-stakes instructions, retain prerequisites, scope, and stop conditions.

## Match the requested register

“Simple,” “brief,” and playful compressed personas are different tones. Follow the user's tone request without caricature, mockery, or loss of professionalism unless they explicitly want informality.
