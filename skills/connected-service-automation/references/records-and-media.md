# Records and media

## Notes, files, and folders

Preserve titles, content, hierarchy, ownership, format, and timestamps when they matter. Search before creating duplicates. For edits, identify whether the service replaces, appends, merges, or creates a new revision. For moves, confirm both source and destination; for deletion, prefer recoverable trash behavior and report recovery limits.

Paginate listings and state whether results are complete. Treat search as a possibly fuzzy or partial operation unless the service guarantees otherwise.

## Media and devices

Resolve the playback account and device before changing state. Distinguish search results from the selected item and report ambiguous matches. Queue, volume, device transfer, and playback commands affect a shared physical environment; avoid surprising changes and verify the final device and status.

Service-specific identifiers and command syntax belong in current tool documentation, not in durable cross-service rules.
