# XR

## Prioritize comfort and safety

Maintain stable frame timing and low motion-to-photon latency. Avoid uncommanded camera movement, abrupt acceleration, horizon instability, and forced near-field focus. Offer locomotion, turning, vignette, seated/standing, handedness, and height options appropriate to the experience.

## Design embodied interaction

Account for reach, hand pose, occlusion, controller tracking, dominant hand, physical fatigue, and uncertain collisions. Use generous targets and multimodal feedback. Keep critical interaction within comfortable zones and provide recovery when objects leave reach.

## Respect physical space

Handle boundary warnings, recentering, passthrough, tracking loss, and transitions between real and virtual context. For AR, account for lighting, surface uncertainty, scale, privacy, and shared-space alignment.

## Test on device

Editor simulation cannot validate comfort, tracking, depth, scale, or fatigue. Test representative sessions on target hardware with different bodies and spaces. Record discomfort, dropped frames, interaction failures, and adaptation time.
